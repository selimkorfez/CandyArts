About Candy Arts CIC

Making Creativity Accessible

Candy Arts is a not-for-profit Community Interest Corporation dedicated to breaking down barriers to creative education. We believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality creative learning opportunities, regardless of their financial situation.

Our mission is to empower individuals through creative skills development, helping them build confidence, discover new talents, and create pathways to employment or further education.

We also support other charities and community groups with affordable video production, graphic design, and web design services, helping them amplify their impact in their communities.

Our Services

🎓

Creative Workshops

Accredited and non-accredited workshops covering digital arts, video production, graphic design, and more. Designed specifically for unemployed individuals and those on low incomes.

🎬

Video Production

Professional video production services at special rates for charities, not-for-profit organizations, and community groups. Help tell your story with impact.

🎨

Graphic Design

Creative graphic design solutions including branding, marketing materials, and digital assets. Affordable rates for organizations making a difference.

💻

Web Design

Modern, accessible website design and development services. We help charities and community groups establish a strong online presence.

📚

Educational Programs

Structured learning pathways that combine creative skills with employability training, helping participants transition into creative careers.

🤝

Community Outreach

We work directly with communities to identify needs and develop targeted programs that address local challenges through creative solutions.
